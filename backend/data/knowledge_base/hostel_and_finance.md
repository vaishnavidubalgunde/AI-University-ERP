# University Fee Schedules, Hostel Guidelines & Financial Regulations

## 1. Tuition Fee Payment Schedules & Fine Slabs
1.1. Tuition and university academic fees for the odd semester must be paid on or before July 31st. Fees for the even semester must be paid on or before January 15th.
1.2. Late fee payment fine slabs:
- Up to 7 days post due date: Grace period (no late fine).
- 8 to 15 days delay: Rs. 500 fixed late fine.
- 16 to 30 days delay: Rs. 1,500 fixed late fine.
- Beyond 30 days delay: Rs. 3,000 late fine and temporary freeze of student portal access.
1.3. Fee receipts are digitally generated upon payment and can be downloaded from the Finance & Accounts section of the ERP.

## 2. Official Document Issuance & Verification Fees
2.1. Bonafide Certificate: Free of charge for first 3 copies per academic year; subsequent copies Rs. 100 each.
2.2. Official Grade Transcript / Consolidated Mark Sheet: Rs. 300 per semester copy.
2.3. No-Dues Clearance Certificate: Issued automatically upon clearance of all library, hostel, lab, and tuition accounts.

## 3. Hostel Residency & Mess Regulations
3.1. Hostel room allotment is conducted at the beginning of each academic year based on merit and distance from hometown.
3.2. Hostel curfew hours: 09:30 PM for undergraduate students. Night-out permissions must be applied through the digital ERP at least 24 hours in advance and require automated OTP verification by registered parent/guardian.
3.3. Mess rebate policy: Students away from hostel for continuous periods exceeding 5 calendar days with approved leave are eligible for a 75% mess rebate credited to the subsequent month invoice.
