# University Academic Regulations & Evaluation Guidelines (2025-2026)

## 1. Credit Structure & Degree Requirements
1.1. The B.Tech degree requires the completion of a minimum of 160 academic credits across 8 semesters.
1.2. Each course is allocated a specific credit weighting based on lecture, tutorial, and practical contact hours (L:T:P).
1.3. A regular student must register for between 18 and 26 credits in any regular academic semester.

## 2. Attendance Policy & Minimum Threshold
2.1. Every student is mandatorily required to maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered course (lectures, tutorials, and laboratories independently).
2.2. Students with attendance between 65% and 74.9% may apply for Condonation of Attendance on valid medical grounds, supported by authenticated hospital documentation submitted within 7 days of discharge.
2.3. Condonation requires the approval of the Academic Council and payment of a statutory condonation fee of Rs. 1,000 per subject.
2.4. Students having attendance below 65% in any course will be detained from appearing in the Semester End Examination (SEE) for that specific course and awarded an 'NS' (Not Satisfied Attendance) grade. They must re-register for the course in a subsequent semester or summer term.

## 3. Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) & Semester End Exams (SEE)
3.1. Total marks for each course: 100 Marks (50% CIE + 50% SEE weightage).
3.2. CIE components include two mid-term examinations (30 marks average), quizzes/assignments (10 marks), and course project/laboratory practicals (10 marks).
3.3. Minimum eligibility to appear for SEE: A student must secure a minimum of 40% (20 out of 50 marks) in the CIE component of the course.

## 4. Grading System & Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)
4.1. Letter grades and Grade Points:
- O (Outstanding): 90-100% -> 10.0 Grade Points
- A+ (Excellent): 80-89% -> 9.0 Grade Points
- A (Very Good): 70-79% -> 8.0 Grade Points
- B+ (Good): 60-69% -> 7.0 Grade Points
- B (Above Average): 55-59% -> 6.0 Grade Points
- C (Average): 50-54% -> 5.0 Grade Points
- P (Pass): 40-49% -> 4.0 Grade Points
- F (Fail): Below 40% -> 0.0 Grade Points

4.2. SGPA Formula: SGPA = Sum(Course Credit * Grade Points) / Sum(Total Semester Credits)
4.3. CGPA Formula: CGPA = Cumulative Sum(Course Credit * Grade Points) / Cumulative Sum(Total Registered Credits)
