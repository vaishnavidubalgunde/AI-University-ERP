# University Examination Ordinance & Hall Ticket Regulations

## 1. Eligibility Criteria for Semester End Examinations (SEE)
1.1. A student is eligible to receive a Digital Hall Ticket and sit for the Semester End Examinations only if:
- They satisfy the minimum 75% attendance criteria (or approved medical condonation) in the respective subject.
- They achieve at least 40% (20/50 marks) in Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE).
- They have cleared all outstanding university tuition, hostel, and laboratory fee dues.
- No disciplinary action or exam debarment is pending against them.

## 2. Digital Hall Ticket & Cryptographic Verification
2.1. Eligible students must download their official Digital Hall Ticket from the ERP portal 7 days prior to the commencement of examinations.
2.2. The digital hall ticket contains a verifiable SHA-256 cryptographic QR code token containing student ID, seat allocation number, examination block, and approved subject codes.
2.3. Hall tickets must be presented to the Chief Superintendent or Invigilator alongside the physical university photo ID card for entry into the exam hall.

## 3. Examination Timetable & Seating Allocation
3.1. Morning session examinations are held from 09:30 AM to 12:30 PM.
3.2. Afternoon session examinations are held from 02:00 PM to 05:00 PM.
3.3. Students must report to their assigned examination room/hall at least 20 minutes prior to the scheduled examination start time. No candidate will be admitted 30 minutes after the commencement of the exam.

## 4. Re-evaluation & Supplementary Makeup Examinations
4.1. Students may apply for photocopy of evaluated answer scripts within 5 working days of result declaration upon payment of Rs. 500 per paper.
4.2. Re-evaluation requests must be filed within 10 working days upon payment of Rs. 1,200 per paper. If marks improve by >= 15%, 50% of the re-evaluation fee is refunded.
4.3. Makeup / Supplementary examinations are conducted annually in July for students with backlogs or approved medical absentees.
