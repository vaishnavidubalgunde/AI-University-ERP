# University Placement Policy & Campus Recruitment Norms (2025-2026)

## 1. Placement Registration & Eligibility Thresholds
1.1. All final-year and pre-final-year students registered with the Department of Corporate Relations & Placements are eligible to participate in on-campus recruitment drives.
1.2. Academic Thresholds:
- Tier-1 / Super Dream Companies (CTC > 20 LPA): Minimum CGPA of 8.00 or above, no active backlogs throughout the academic program.
- Dream Companies (CTC 10 LPA to 20 LPA): Minimum CGPA of 7.00 to 7.50, maximum 1 historical cleared backlog allowed.
- Core & Mass Recruiters (CTC up to 10 LPA): Minimum CGPA of 6.00 to 6.50, up to 2 historical cleared backlogs permitted.

## 2. One Student - One Offer Policy (and Upgrade Rules)
2.1. Once a candidate secures an offer from a company with a package <= 10 LPA, they are considered 'Placed' and withdrawn from further drives with equivalent packages.
2.2. Dream Upgrade Rule: A student with an existing regular offer is permitted to interview for up to 3 Dream or Super Dream companies offering at least a 1.5x CTC increment over their existing offer.
2.3. Super Dream Upgrade: Any student securing an offer between 10 LPA and 20 LPA may appear for companies offering > 25 LPA.
2.4. Once an offer from a Super Dream company (> 20 LPA) is accepted, the candidate is strictly ineligible for further campus drives.

## 3. Code of Conduct & Interview Protocol
3.1. Strict adherence to professional formal dress code is mandatory during pre-placement talks (PPTs), technical interviews, and HR discussions.
3.2. Failure to attend an interview round after being shortlisted without prior written approval from the Placement Officer will result in a 3-drive debarment penalty.
3.3. Dual acceptance of conflicting offers without placement cell consent will lead to immediate cancellation of all campus recruitment privileges and disciplinary notation.
